# Product Principles

ETERPAX is built around five non-negotiable principles.

## 1. Calm

Every screen should reduce anxiety.

If a screen creates stress, confusion, or uncertainty, it must be redesigned.

---

## 2. Trust

Users must feel safe before they understand the technology.

Design communicates trust before words do.

---

## 3. Clarity

The user should never wonder:

"What do I do next?"

---

## 4. Human First

We never build features because they are technically interesting.

We build features because they solve real human problems.

---

## 5. Premium Simplicity

Our goal is not minimalism.

Our goal is effortless understanding.

Simple does not mean empty.

Premium does not mean complicated.
